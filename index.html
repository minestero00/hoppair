A few useful details.

Do I need the same Wi-Fi?

Use the same local network for direct transfers. An optional self-hosted relay connects devices across networks. The download does not include a public relay.

Where does my data go?

The shelf is stored on your devices. Transfers use AES-GCM encryption. The relay forwards encrypted traffic and does not store your files. Keep pairing QR codes and links private.

What are the current limits?

One Mac and one Android phone. Up to 8 MiB per file, 64 KiB per text item, and 500 items. Android syncs while open. Full shelves reject new items. Item deletion and in-app pairing-key rotation are not available yet.

Can I help improve it?

Please do. Hoppair is MIT licensed. Contribute a focused improvement, report a bug, or share a useful idea.
